I am a first-year PhD student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by [Prof. Yao Mu](https://yaomarkmu.github.io/).

I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Future Technology at Harbin Institute of Technology, where I was advised by [Prof. Wangmeng Zuo](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=rUOpCEYAAAAJ&hl=en). During my undergraduate studies, I worked on monocular 4D reconstruction of hand–object interactions involving articulated objects.

My current research focuses on embodied pretraining for dexterous manipulation. At Sharpa Robotics, I build the full data pipeline that turns large-scale egocentric human videos and robot data into pretraining data for embodied foundation models, and I also work on agentic AI.
